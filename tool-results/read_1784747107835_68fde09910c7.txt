    41→    41→# SECTION 1: DESIGN PHILOSOPHY & PRINCIPLES
    42→    42→
    43→    43→## Core Philosophy
    44→    44→
    45→    45→The platform must convey trust, authority, and editorial excellence at first glance. Every design decision — from typeface selection to the spacing between article cards — should serve the reader's experience, not the brand's ego.
    46→    46→
    47→    47→Think of the reading experience as the primary product. Every other element exists to support that core function.
    48→    48→
    49→    49→## Design Principles
    50→    50→
    51→    51→### 1. Editorial First
    52→    52→Content is the hero. The UI recedes to give maximum visual weight to headlines, images, and text. Chrome (buttons, borders, backgrounds) is minimal and purposeful. Never let decoration compete with journalism.
    53→    53→
    54→    54→### 2. Trust Through Restraint
    55→    55→Premium news publications earn trust by appearing composed and disciplined. Avoid flashy animations, aggressive color blocking, or gimmicky layouts. Use whitespace generously — it signals confidence.
    56→    56→
    57→    57→### 3. Hierarchy by Importance
    58→    58→Every screen must communicate an order of importance at a glance. The reader's eye should flow naturally from the most significant story to the least. Achieve this through typography scale, contrast, and spatial positioning — not color noise.
    59→    59→
    60→    60→### 4. Speed as a Feature
    61→    61→A slow site destroys reader trust. Every layout decision should account for perceived performance. Skeleton screens, lazy loading, and progressive rendering are not afterthoughts — they are first-class design features.
    62→    62→
    63→    63→### 5. Mobile as Primary
    64→    64→The majority of news consumption happens on mobile. All layouts are designed mobile-first and then scaled up, not the reverse.
    65→    65→
    66→    66→### 6. Accessibility as Quality
    67→    67→A design that excludes any reader is an incomplete design. Contrast ratios, keyboard navigation, and screen reader support are not optional enhancements — they are baseline requirements.

   222→   222→# SECTION 4: NAVIGATION STRUCTURE
   223→   223→
   224→   224→## 4.1 Primary Navigation (Desktop)
   225→   225→
   226→   226→The primary nav sits in a fixed header at the top of all public pages. It does not scroll away.
   227→   227→
   228→   228→**Left side:** Logo (text or SVG mark). Clicking returns to homepage.
   229→   229→
   230→   230→**Center:** Main navigation links. The recommended order:
   231→   231→`Home | Live | Breaking | National | International | State News | Podcasts`
   232→   232→
   233→   233→**Right side:** Search icon (expands to search bar inline), optional language toggle if multilingual support is planned.
   234→   234→
   235→   235→**Secondary navigation bar** (below primary): A horizontally scrollable category pill row.
   236→   236→`Politics | Economy | Sports | Health | Technology | Science | Entertainment | [more...]`
   237→   237→
   238→   238→This bar scrolls independently and does not wrap to multiple lines.
   239→   239→

   276→   276→
   277→   277→```

   280→   280→        → Article Detail Page loads
   281→   281→        → Reads article
   282→   282→        → Sees Related News at bottom
   283→   283→        → Clicks related article (new article loads)

   288→   288→## 5.2 Public Visitor Flow — Live News
   289→   289→
   290→   290→```

   294→   294→         → Player autoplays (muted by default)

   366→   366→# SECTION 6: HOMEPAGE BLUEPRINT
   367→   367→
   368→   368→The homepage is the platform's front page — it must communicate authority, breadth of coverage, and editorial judgment in a single scroll.
   369→   369→
   370→   370→## 6.1 Announcement Bar (Top, optional)
   371→   371→
   372→   372→**Purpose:** Alerts for critical events — election results, major breaking news, site maintenance.
   373→   373→
   374→   374→**Layout:** Full-width, single-line, colored background (use sparingly — bright amber or deep red). Centered text. Close (×) button on far right.
   375→   375→
   376→   376→**Behavior:** Dismissible per session. Controlled by admin toggle. Disappears when no active announcement.
   377→   377→
   378→   378→**Responsive:** Same on all breakpoints. Collapses gracefully on very small screens (reduce font size).
   379→   379→
   380→   380→---
   381→   381→
   382→   382→## 6.2 Header
   383→   383→
   384→   384→**Purpose:** Brand identity + primary navigation access on every page.
   385→   385→
   386→   386→**Layout:** Fixed (sticky) to top of viewport. Full width. Slightly translucent with backdrop blur when page is scrolled. Solid background when at top.
   387→   387→
   388→   388→**Desktop:** Logo left, Nav center, Search + utility icons right. Height: 64px.
   389→   389→
   390→   390→**Mobile:** Logo left, Search + Hamburger right. Height: 56px.
   391→   391→
   392→   392→**Visual:** Thin 1px bottom border in neutral tone. Subtle shadow on scroll.
   393→   393→
   394→   394→---
   395→   395→
   396→   396→## 6.3 Navigation Bar
   397→   397→
   398→   398→**Purpose:** Section and category access.
   399→   399→
   400→   400→**Desktop Layout:** Horizontal link row below header. Uses the full-width secondary bar for categories (scrollable). Active section indicated by bottom border underline, not background fill.
   401→   401→
   402→   402→**Mobile Layout:** Condensed. Category pills hidden behind "more" arrow or accessed via drawer.
   403→   403→
   404→   404→---
   405→   405→
   406→   406→## 6.4 Breaking News Ticker
   407→   407→
   408→   408→**Purpose:** Communicate urgency. Alert readers to developing stories.
   409→   409→
   410→   410→**Layout:** Full-width band immediately below the nav. Dark background (near-black or deep red). White text. Label on left: "BREAKING" in bold caps. Ticker text scrolls right-to-left. Multiple stories separated by a bullet (•) or pipe (|).
   411→   411→
   412→   412→**Behavior:** Auto-scrolls continuously. On hover (desktop) or tap (mobile), scroll pauses. Each headline is tappable — navigates to that article.
   413→   413→
   414→   414→**Admin control:** Super Admin can enable/disable the ticker and edit the list of breaking stories.
   415→   415→
   416→   416→**Responsive:** Same on all breakpoints. On very small screens, reduce label to icon only (red alert icon).
   417→   417→
   418→   418→---
   419→   419→
   420→   420→## 6.5 Featured Story (Hero)
   421→   421→
   422→   422→**Purpose:** The single most important story of the moment. Editorial showcase.
   423→   423→
   424→   424→**Layout — Desktop:**
   425→   425→Full-width panel. Left two-thirds: Large hero image. Right one-third: Category label, large headline (H1 scale), standfirst/summary paragraph (2–3 lines), author name, timestamp, "Read More" CTA link.
   426→   426→
   427→   427→Alternatively: Full-bleed hero image with gradient overlay darkening bottom half. Text overlaid on gradient: category badge, headline, standfirst.
   428→   428→
   429→   429→**Layout — Mobile:**
   430→   430→Image on top (16:9 or 3:2 ratio). Text block below. Headline scales down (H2 size). Standfirst truncated or hidden.
   431→   431→
   432→   432→**Visual hierarchy:** Headline is the largest text on the page. Category badge uses accent color. Timestamp in muted small text.
   433→   433→
   434→   434→**Interaction:** Entire card is clickable. No button needed — the cursor changes to pointer on hover.
   435→   435→
   436→   436→**Admin control:** Super Admin manually designates the featured story from the published pool.
   437→   437→
   438→   438→---
   439→   439→
   440→   440→## 6.6 Top Stories Grid
   441→   441→
   442→   442→**Purpose:** Showcase the next 4–6 highest-priority stories below the hero.
   443→   443→
   444→   444→**Layout — Desktop:**
   445→   445→3-column grid. Left column: 1 large card (larger image + headline + standfirst). Center and right columns: 2–3 smaller cards stacked (smaller image + headline + metadata only).
   446→   446→
   447→   447→Or: A clean 3-column equal grid of medium cards.
   448→   448→
   449→   449→**Layout — Tablet:** 2-column grid.
   450→   450→
   451→   451→**Layout — Mobile:** Single column, stacked cards. Horizontal scrollable row as an alternative for "Top Stories" label section.
   452→   452→
   453→   453→**Card anatomy:**
   454→   454→- Image (aspect ratio 16:9, lazy loaded)
   455→   455→- Category label (colored badge, top-left on image or above headline)
   456→   456→- Headline (H3 size)
   457→   457→- Author + Timestamp (small, muted)
   458→   458→- Optional: Reading time label
   459→   459→
   460→   460→**Interaction:** Entire card clickable. Image has subtle zoom on hover (desktop). Cards have very slight lift shadow on hover.
   461→   461→
   462→   462→---
   463→   463→
   464→   464→## 6.7 Latest News Feed
   465→   465→
   466→   466→**Purpose:** Real-time freshness. Shows the most recently published articles across all categories.
   467→   467→
   468→   468→**Layout — Desktop:** 1 column of compact list items with timestamp on the left and headline on the right. OR a 2-column masonry-lite grid of compact cards.
   469→   469→
   470→   470→**Layout — Mobile:** Single-column list of text-only headline items with small thumbnail on the right.
   471→   471→
   472→   472→**Update behavior:** New articles prepend to top. A subtle "New stories available — Tap to refresh" banner appears when new content is added without forcing a full page reload.
   473→   473→
   474→   474→**Label:** "LATEST NEWS" with a small animated green dot (live pulse animation) beside it.
   475→   475→
   476→   476→---
   477→   477→
   478→   478→## 6.8 Category Blocks
   479→   479→
   480→   480→**Purpose:** Organized editorial browsing by topic.
   481→   481→
   482→   482→**Layout:** 2–3 horizontal "ribbon" sections, each dedicated to one category (e.g., Politics, Economy, Sports). Each ribbon has:
   483→   483→- Section label on the left with "View All →" link on the right
   484→   484→- Horizontal row of 3–4 cards (scrollable on mobile)
   485→   485→
   486→   486→**Card size:** Medium. Image + headline + timestamp.
   487→   487→
   488→   488→**Behavior on mobile:** Each category block becomes a horizontal scroll container (swipeable card row).
   489→   489→
   490→   490→---
   491→   491→
   492→   492→## 6.9 State-Wise News
   493→   493→
   494→   494→**Purpose:** Serve readers who want news from their specific state.
   495→   495→
   496→   496→**Layout — Desktop:** 2-column. Left: State selector (vertical list of state names or a dropdown). Right: 3–5 article cards for the selected state.
   497→   497→
   498→   498→**Layout — Mobile:** State selector as a horizontal pill scroll at top. Below: article list updates when state is selected.
   499→   499→
   500→   500→**Default state:** Auto-detect or show a default popular state. User's selection persists for the session.
   501→   501→
   502→   502→**Interaction:** Clicking a state immediately updates the article panel without a page reload (filter interaction).
   503→   503→
   504→   504→---
   505→   505→
   506→   506→## 6.10 Live News Section
   507→   507→
   508→   508→**Purpose:** Promote the live broadcast with urgency.
   509→   509→
   510→   510→**Layout:** Full-width section with dark or brand-color background to differentiate it from surrounding content. Contains:
   511→   511→- "LIVE NOW" badge with pulsing red dot
   512→   512→- Thumbnail of current YouTube live stream (16:9)
   513→   513→- Title of the live program

  1402→  1402→
  1403→  1403→### Font Recommendations
  1404→  1404→- **Heading Font:** A premium serif for authority (e.g., Playfair Display, Lora) or a high-quality editorial sans (e.g., Source Serif, Fraunces). The heading font defines the editorial personality.
  1405→  1405→- **Body Font:** A highly legible news text font — optimized for long reading. (e.g., Georgia, Merriweather, Source Serif Pro)
  1406→  1406→- **UI Font:** A clean sans-serif for all UI chrome — navigation, labels, buttons, metadata. (e.g., Inter, DM Sans, Work Sans)
  1407→  1407→
  1408→  1408→### Font Pairing Rule
  1409→  1409→Heading font (serif) + UI font (sans-serif) is the recommended pairing for a premium editorial feel. Body can match heading or have a separate companion serif.

  1588→  1588→# SECTION 20: ANIMATION BLUEPRINT
  1589→  1589→
  1590→  1590→## 20.1 Animation Philosophy
  1591→  1591→
  1592→  1592→Animations serve communication, not entertainment. Every animation on this platform should reduce cognitive load, not add to it. Animations are used to:
  1593→  1593→- Orient the user (where did this element come from? where is it going?)
  1594→  1594→- Confirm interactions (the button responded)
  1595→  1595→- Signal loading states (something is happening)
  1596→  1596→- Aid transitions (we're moving to a new context)
  1597→  1597→
  1598→  1598→If an animation doesn't serve one of these four purposes, it is not needed.
  1599→  1599→
  1600→  1600→## 20.2 Animation Timing
  1601→  1601→
  1602→  1602→| Type | Duration | Easing |
  1603→  1603→|---|---|---|
  1604→  1604→| Micro-interactions (hover, press) | 150–200ms | `ease-out` |
  1605→  1605→| Element entrance (fade/slide in) | 250–350ms | `ease-out` |
  1606→  1606→| Page transitions | 300–400ms | `ease-in-out` |
  1607→  1607→| Drawers & modals | 300ms | `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` |
  1608→  1608→| Skeleton shimmer | 1500ms | Looping linear |
  1609→  1609→| Breaking ticker scroll | 20–40s (loop) | Linear |
  1610→  1610→| Live dot pulse | 2s | Looping ease-in-out |
  1611→  1611→
  1612→  1612→---
  1613→  1613→
  1614→  1614→## 20.3 Page Transitions
  1615→  1615→
  1616→  1616→When navigating between pages, the content area performs a quick fade-in (opacity 0→1 over 200ms) as the new page renders. Avoid slide transitions between pages as they can feel disorienting in a content-rich environment.
  1617→  1617→
  1618→  1618→On mobile, article open transitions: the article content fades in with a very slight upward translate (8–12px, 250ms). This simulates a "lifting" effect without being theatrical.
  1619→  1619→
  1620→  1620→---
  1621→  1621→
  1622→  1622→## 20.4 Scroll Animations
  1623→  1623→
  1624→  1624→Elements entering the viewport (article cards below the fold, section headings) fade in with a subtle upward translate of 16px over 300ms. This is triggered once — elements do not animate every time they enter the viewport.
  1625→  1625→
  1626→  1626→The Breaking News ticker scrolls continuously at a speed calibrated to be readable (~150 pixels per second). It loops seamlessly.
  1627→  1627→
  1628→  1628→The Reading Progress bar animates in real-time as the user scrolls — no easing or delay. It must feel perfectly in sync with scroll position.
  1629→  1629→
  1630→  1630→---
  1631→  1631→
  1632→  1632→## 20.5 Hover Animations (Desktop)
  1633→  1633→
  1634→  1634→- **Article card image:** Scale from 1.0 to 1.03 over 300ms. Overflow hidden on the card so the image scales within its container.
  1635→  1635→- **Article card elevation:** Shadow increases from Level 0 to Level 2 over 200ms.
  1636→  1636→- **Navigation links:** Underline animates from left to right over 200ms.
  1637→  1637→- **Buttons:** Background color shift over 150ms.
  1638→  1638→- **Category badges:** Subtle background fill appears over 150ms.
  1639→  1639→- **Accordion chevron:** Rotates 180° over 200ms when open/close toggled.
  1640→  1640→
  1641→  1641→---
  1642→  1642→
  1643→  1643→## 20.6 Loading Animations
  1644→  1644→
  1645→  1645→**Skeleton screens:** All card grids, article feeds, and list views show skeleton placeholders while loading. Skeleton elements match the exact layout of real content. A smooth shimmer (a lighter highlight that moves left-to-right) indicates active loading.
  1646→  1646→
  1647→  1647→**Image loading:** Use a low-quality blurred placeholder (LQIP) that transitions to full resolution as the image loads. No jarring "pop" of content.
  1648→  1648→
  1649→  1649→**Button loading state:** When a form is submitting, the button shows a small inline spinner and its label changes to "Submitting…". The button is disabled during this state.
  1650→  1650→
  1651→  1651→---
  1652→  1652→
  1653→  1653→## 20.7 Micro-interactions
  1654→  1654→
  1655→  1655→- **Search icon tap (mobile):** The overlay slides down from the top with a 250ms ease-out.
  1656→  1656→- **Share button tap:** Action sheet slides up from bottom (mobile) in 250ms.
  1657→  1657→- **Audio play/pause:** The play icon transitions to a pause icon with a quick cross-fade, not an abrupt switch.
  1658→  1658→- **Newsletter subscribe success:** The input field and button transition to a success message — the form elements fade out as the success text fades in.
  1659→  1659→- **Accordion open:** Height animates from 0 to auto (use max-height technique for CSS, or JS-measured for precision). Content fades in simultaneously.
  1660→  1660→- **Toast appearance:** Slides in from bottom-right (desktop) or bottom-center (mobile). Slides back out on dismiss.
  1661→  1661→
  1662→  1662→---
  1663→  1663→
  1664→  1664→## 20.8 Modal & Drawer Animations
  1665→  1665→
  1666→  1666→**Modal:** Background overlay fades to 50% opacity (200ms). Modal panel scales from 97% to 100% and fades in (250ms). Combined effect is subtle and elegant.
  1667→  1667→
  1668→  1668→**Mobile Drawer (menu):** Slides in from the right edge of the screen over 300ms. Backdrop darkens simultaneously. Swipe-right to close also at 300ms.
  1669→  1669→
  1670→  1670→**Bottom Sheet (mobile):** Slides up from the bottom over 300ms. Spring physics are not recommended here — use simple ease-out for a premium, controlled feel.
  1671→  1671→
  1672→  1672→---
  1673→  1673→
  1674→  1674→## 20.9 Reduced Motion
  1675→  1675→
  1676→  1676→All animations respect the `prefers-reduced-motion` media query. When enabled:
  1677→  1677→- Page transitions are instant (no fade)
  1678→  1678→- Scroll animations are disabled
  1679→  1679→- Hover effects are instant (no transition duration)
  1680→  1680→- Skeleton shimmer is static (no movement)
  1681→  1681→- Ticker scrolls at half speed or is replaced with a static display

  1689→  1689→## 21.1 Color Contrast
  1690→  1690→

  1692→  1692→- Large text (heading, display): minimum 4.5:1
  1693→  1693→- UI components (button labels, input borders): minimum 3:1
  1694→  1694→- Category badges: text on badge background must meet 4.5:1
  1695→  1695→- Error text on white: must meet 4.5:1
  1696→  1696→

  1703→  1703→- No text smaller than 12px in the published interface (12px is an absolute minimum for micro-labels)

  1713→  1713→- Tab order must match the logical visual flow of the page (top-left to bottom-right)
  1714→  1714→- Modal dialogs must trap focus inside when open
  1715→  1715→- Dropdown menus must be navigable with arrow keys; Escape closes them
  1716→  1716→- Skip navigation link: A "Skip to main content" link is the first focusable element on every page, hidden until focused

  1723→  1723→- Focus indicators must never be removed with `outline: none` without a custom replacement
  1724→  1724→- Focus indicators must meet 3:1 contrast ratio against adjacent colors

  1729→  1729→
  1730→  1730→- All images must have meaningful `alt` text. Decorative images use `alt=""`
  1731→  1731→- The reading progress bar must be marked `aria-hidden="true"` (decorative)

  1733→  1733→- Live status indicators ("LIVE", "BREAKING") must convey meaning via text, not color alone
  1734→  1734→- Audio player controls must have ARIA labels (`aria-label="Play episode"`, etc.)
  1735→  1735→- Article cards must be implemented as links (`<a>`) wrapping the entire card, or as individual link elements with meaningful link text
  1736→  1736→- Navigation landmark roles: `<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, `<aside>`, `<footer>` must all be used correctly
  1737→  1737→- Forms: All inputs must have associated `<label>` elements (not just placeholder text)

  1744→  1744→- Targets should have at least 8px spacing between adjacent interactive elements to prevent mis-taps
  1745→  1745→- The bottom navigation bar items must each have the full height of the bar as a touch target

  1770→  1770→# SECTION 22: PERFORMANCE BLUEPRINT
  1771→  1771→
  1772→  1772→## 22.1 Loading Philosophy
  1773→  1773→
  1774→  1774→The user's first impression is formed in the first 2 seconds. If the site feels slow in that window, trust is damaged before any content has been read. Performance is a design deliverable, not a technical afterthought.
  1775→  1775→
  1776→  1776→## 22.2 Core Web Vitals Targets
  1777→  1777→
  1778→  1778→| Metric | Target |
  1779→  1779→|---|---|
  1780→  1780→| LCP (Largest Contentful Paint) | < 2.0 seconds |
  1781→  1781→| FID / INP (Interaction Delay) | < 100ms |
  1782→  1782→| CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift) | < 0.1 |
  1783→  1783→| TTFB (Time to First Byte) | < 600ms |
  1784→  1784→
  1785→  1785→---
  1786→  1786→
  1787→  1787→## 22.3 Image Optimization
  1788→  1788→
  1789→  1789→- All images served in modern formats: WebP primary, JPEG fallback
  1790→  1790→- Responsive images using `srcset` and `sizes` — the browser downloads only the resolution needed for the device
  1791→  1791→- Hero images: preloaded (`<link rel="preload">`) to improve LCP
  1792→  1792→- All images below the fold: lazy loaded (`loading="lazy"`)
  1793→  1793→- No images with intrinsic dimensions left unspecified (always set `width` and `height` attributes to prevent CLS)
  1794→  1794→- Low-quality image placeholders (LQIP) for perceived performance
  1795→  1795→
  1796→  1796→---
  1797→  1797→
  1798→  1798→## 22.4 Skeleton Screens
  1799→  1799→
  1800→  1800→Every list, grid, and feed shows skeleton placeholder elements during the data fetch. Skeletons:
  1801→  1801→- Match the exact layout of the real content
  1802→  1802→- Use an animated shimmer effect
  1803→  1803→- Never show a spinning loader as a replacement for content layout (spinners cause CLS and feel slower)
  1804→  1804→
  1805→  1805→---
  1806→  1806→
  1807→  1807→## 22.5 Lazy Loading
  1808→  1808→
  1809→  1809→- Images below the fold: `loading="lazy"`
  1810→  1810→- Comments section (if added): loaded only when user scrolls near it
  1811→  1811→- Advertisement scripts: loaded asynchronously, deferred until after main content
  1812→  1812→- Third-party embeds (YouTube, etc.): facade pattern — show a thumbnail with a play button; the iframe only loads when the user clicks play
  1813→  1813→
  1814→  1814→---
  1815→  1815→
  1816→  1816→## 22.6 Infinite Scrolling vs. Pagination
  1817→  1817→
  1818→  1818→- **Homepage Latest News feed:** Infinite scroll (or "Load More" button as a hybrid) — users expect continuous scrolling for news feeds
  1819→  1819→- **Search results:** Paginated with "Load More" — allows users to maintain position when navigating back from a result
  1820→  1820→- **Category pages:** "Load More" button preferred — gives users control over the loading moment
  1821→  1821→- All infinite scroll implementations must include a "You've reached the end" state and "Back to Top" affordance
  1822→  1822→
  1823→  1823→---
  1824→  1824→
  1825→  1825→## 22.7 Caching Strategy
  1826→  1826→
  1827→  1827→- Static assets (CSS, JS, fonts, logo): aggressively cached with long TTL (1 year, with content hash in filename for cache-busting on updates)
  1828→  1828→- Article pages: short-to-medium cache (5–15 minutes) with stale-while-revalidate pattern
  1829→  1829→- Breaking news ticker and live updates: no caching (always fresh)
  1830→  1830→- Homepage: short cache (60–120 seconds) with background revalidation
  1831→  1831→
  1832→  1832→---
  1833→  1833→
  1834→  1834→## 22.8 Font Loading
  1835→  1835→
  1836→  1836→- Use `font-display: swap` for all web fonts to prevent invisible text during load
  1837→  1837→- Preload the heading font and body font `woff2` files in the document `<head>`
  1838→  1838→- Limit the number of font files: 2 typefaces maximum, each in 2–3 weights. More fonts = more network requests = slower load.
  1839→  1839→- System font stack as fallback while web fonts load (ensure the system font is dimensionally similar to the target font to prevent CLS from font swap)
  1840→  1840→

  1843→  1843→## 22.9 Third-Party Scripts
  1844→  1844→
  1845→  1845→- All third-party scripts (analytics, ads) loaded asynchronously
  1846→  1846→- No third-party scripts in the critical rendering path
  1847→  1847→- Ad scripts use `async` and `defer` attributes
  1848→  1848→- YouTube player uses facade (poster + play button) until interaction — prevents the YouTube iframe from blocking page load
